Bronx × Manhattan · urban heat

Risk of Simply Standing Still

The climate-change conversation often prioritizes acute disasters over the slow, inevitable threat of rising urban heat. Hotter summers are something we have experienced consistently over the past five years, with each one setting a new record.

In the Bronx, this crisis is especially severe. The map shows that large portions of neighborhoods fall outside the walking radius of subway stations, forcing residents to rely on unreliable bus networks. This means prolonged exposure to extreme heat at poorly maintained stops — a daily risk that will only worsen.

While subway expansion is necessary, it is a decades-long process. This analysis identifies priority zones requiring immediate, tactical interventions — for example, reimagined bus stops with enhanced shading, cooling infrastructure, and public amenities that respond to current conditions and offer more immediate relief for transit-dependent communities facing escalating urban heat.
